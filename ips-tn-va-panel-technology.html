TFT Panel Technologies: TN, IPS and VA

Overview · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Three LC modes

TN twists the liquid crystal 90 degrees between glass plates; IPS switches it in-plane for wide viewing; VA tilts it vertically for deep black. Each trades cost, angle and contrast differently on industrial TFT modules.

Where each fits

  • TN: cheapest and fastest, weaker off-axis colour - fine for simple HMIs.
  • IPS: wide cone and stable colour - best where operators read from angles.
  • VA: highest contrast and black level - good for dark-room UI.

Match the mode to the install; see viewing angles.
