Display Brightness and Contrast Explained

Fundamentals · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Brightness (nits)

Brightness is luminous intensity per area, cd/m² (nits). It sets how the panel reads under ambient light. Indoor HMIs are fine near 300 nits; outdoor and sunlight-readable units climb to 1000 nits and above.

Contrast ratio

Contrast is the ratio of lit to dark pixel luminance. Higher contrast makes text and diagrams pop, especially in mixed lighting. Optical bonding helps by killing surface reflections that wash out dark areas.

Transmissive vs transflective

  • Transmissive: needs a backlight; best indoors, washed out in sun.
  • Transflective: reflects ambient light when backlight is off; readable in daylight, dim indoors.
