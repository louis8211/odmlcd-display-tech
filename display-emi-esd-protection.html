EMI and ESD Protection for Industrial Displays

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Where EMI comes from

Switching power supplies, motor drives, relays and long cable runs all radiate. A display's flat cable, touch sensor and unshielded LVDS link are the usual pickup points on the factory floor.

ESD paths

Static reaches the panel through the cover glass, connectors and operator-touched surfaces. ESD testing on industrial units checks that transient hits do not latch or damage the controller.

Mitigations that work

  • Shielded cables and a grounded bezel / chassis.
  • Ferrite clamps and filtered power at the entry point.
  • TVS diodes and proper return paths on touch and interface lines.
  • Frequency-hopping on capacitive touch to dodge narrowband noise.
