Reading an Industrial LCD Datasheet

Reference · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

A datasheet is the contract between the module and your design. Knowing where to look saves integration weeks. This guide walks the sections that matter for industrial TFT modules.

1. Absolute maximum ratings

Voltage, temperature and storage limits. Design inside these with margin — they are not operating targets.

2. Optical characteristics

SpecWhat it means for you
Brightness (cd/m² / nits)Need more for sunlight — see selection guide
Contrast ratioReadability of dark content
Viewing angleIPS gives wide, stable angles
Colour gamutMatch to your UI / camera needs

3. Electrical & timing

  • Pinout / connector — map it to your controller board.
  • Interface — RGB / LVDS / MIPI / eDP (see interface primer).
  • Timing diagrams — pixel clock, H/V sync, DE; required for a clean image.

4. Mechanical drawing

The outline, bezel, mounting holes and touch-stack thickness. This is what your enclosure is built around — confirm it before anything else.
