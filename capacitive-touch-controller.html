Capacitive Touch Controller ICs

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

What the controller does

The controller scans the sensor matrix, rejects noise and reports coordinates. A capacitive touch module lives or dies on controller firmware quality in noisy fields.

Multi-touch and gestures

Modern controllers report multiple points and basic gestures over a standard protocol. The host UI interprets them; pick a controller with a driver or HID profile your OS already supports.

Host interface and noise

Most report over I2C or USB. Industrial noise demands good grounding and frequency hopping - see touch integration and EMI/ESD protection.
