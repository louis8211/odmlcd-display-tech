LCD Controller Board Selection

Hardware · Companion to the article on odmlcd.com

A TFT panel is dumb glass — it needs a controller board (often called a driver board or TCON-adapter) to accept a video source and drive the pixels. Choosing the wrong one means no image, wrong colours, or a board that will not fit the enclosure.

Match the board to the panel interface

The single most important check is the panel's interface: RGB, LVDS, MIPI DSI, eDP or SPI. The board must speak the same language. See the LCD Interface Primer for the differences.

Decide the input you need

Input sourceTypical boardUse case
HDMI / VGAHDMI driver boardStandalone monitors, kiosks
RGB / MCU (8080/SPI)MCU interface boardEmbedded systems, small panels
LVDS / eDPConverter + SBCIndustrial PCs, custom HMI

Don't forget the mechanical fit

  • Board outline and mounting holes vs. your bezel/enclosure.
  • Inverter / backlight driver current rating matches the panel.
  • Operating temperature range for the components, not just the panel.
