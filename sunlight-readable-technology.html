Sunlight Readable Display Technology

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Why sunlight washes out

A panel is readable only when its own luminance beats ambient reflection. In direct sun, ambient can exceed 1000 lux of reflected glare, so a dim backlight disappears. The fix is more output and less reflection.

High-brightness backlight

Sunlight-readable modules start at 800-1000 nits and go higher, often with a brighter LED string and a sturdier thermal path. More nits directly lifts the panel above ambient glare.

Cut reflection, not just add light

Transflective stacks, anti-reflection surface treatment and optical bonding each remove a reflective interface. Combining them means you need less backlight for the same readability and saves power. See also backlight design.
