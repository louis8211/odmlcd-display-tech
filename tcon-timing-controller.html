T-Con Timing Controller Basics

Reference · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

What the T-Con does

The timing controller receives the LVDS, eDP or MIPI stream and generates the precise row/column drive signals, plus gamma and overdrive. It is the brain between source board and glass.

Input formats

It must match the incoming link; mismatches mean no picture. See the interface primer and LVDS vs eDP for the map.

Gamma and uniformity

Per-column gamma and compensation fix brightness and colour unevenness. Verify with test patterns at incoming inspection.
