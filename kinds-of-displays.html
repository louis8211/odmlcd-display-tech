Kinds of Displays: A Practical Map

Overview · Companion to the article on odmlcd.com

"Display" covers very different technologies. For an industrial design the relevant question is never "which is best" but "which fits the environment, lifetime and interface I have."

Liquid-crystal families

  • TN (Twisted Nematic) — cheapest, fast, but narrow viewing angles and weak colour. Common in basic character/segment displays.
  • IPS (In-Plane Switching) — wide viewing angles and accurate colour; the default for most industrial TFT modules.
  • VA (Vertical Alignment) — high contrast, often used in large-format and automotive contexts.

Emissive and specialty

  • TFT LCD — backlit, mature, long-life; the workhorse for industrial HMI.
  • OLED / AMOLED — self-emissive, see What Is an AMOLED Display.
  • E-paper — bistable, ultra-low power, slow — niche signage only.
  • Round & bar-type TFT — special shapes for meters and appliances; see round/bar TFT modules.
