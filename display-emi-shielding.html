EMI Shielding for Display Assemblies

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Shield the right layer

A thin ITO shield layer on the touch glass, a conductive gasket at the bezel, or a shielded can over the T-con each block radiation at different points. Pick by where the noise leaves.

Cable and connector shield

Shield the cable and connector and the FPC so the link does not become an antenna; grounding at one end avoids loops.

Verify early

Pre-compliance checks catch issues before full EMC testing; reliability tests should include both emissions and immunity.
