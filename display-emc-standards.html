EMC Standards for Industrial Displays

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

What EMC covers

EMC means the display neither emits excess noise nor fails when exposed to it. Regions mandate CE (EN), FCC or equivalents; industrial environments often add stricter immunity.

Emissions vs immunity

Emissions limit what the panel radiates; immunity checks it survives nearby motors and radio. A reliability test plan covers both with the right setup.

Design to pass

Shielded cables, a grounded bezel, filtered power and good touch/interface return paths do most of the work. See EMI and ESD protection.
