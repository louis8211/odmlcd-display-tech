Display Power Consumption Budget

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Backlight dominates

For most TFT modules the backlight is 70-90% of total power. Panel and controller draw little by comparison, so power tuning starts at the LED string - see backlight design.

Low-power modes

Dim the backlight, drop refresh rate, or blank the panel when idle. A wide-temperature module in a battery device benefits most from aggressive dimming and sleep.

Sizing the supply

Size the regulator for max backlight current plus a margin for cold-start and inrush, not the average. Undersizing causes brownouts that the controller reads as reset.
