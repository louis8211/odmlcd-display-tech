Optical Bonding vs Air Gap in Industrial Displays

Reference · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

What optical bonding is

Optical bonding fills the gap between the TFT surface and the cover glass (or touch) with a clear resin or film of matched refractive index. An optically bonded display removes the internal air interface that an ordinary assembly has.

Contrast and sunlight

Each air interface reflects light. Removing it cuts reflections, lifts effective contrast and makes the panel far more readable in bright ambient light - key for outdoor and high-contrast installs.

Ruggedness and condensation

No air gap means no dust or moisture trapped inside, and better shock resistance. It also stops condensation fogging the inner surface in humid, temperature-swing environments.
