Display Power-On Sequencing

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Why sequence matters

Applying backlight or panel voltages out of order can latch the controller or stress the driver IC. A defined ramp protects the glass and silicon.

Typical sequence

  • Logic and I/O power first.
  • Then AVDD and VCOM reference.
  • Finally enable the backlight once the panel is stable.

Reset and brownout

Hold reset low until supplies settle, and design the regulator for inrush so a brownout does not reset mid-frame - tied to the power budget.
