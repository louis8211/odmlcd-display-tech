Integrating Touch Panels with TFT Modules

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Cover glass and bonding

A capacitive touch module stacks a sensor and cover glass in front of the TFT. Optical bonding the touch stack to the panel removes the air gap, cutting reflections and improving ruggedness in the field.

Controller and host interface

Most industrial touch controllers report over I²C or USB. The host reads coordinates and feeds them to the UI. Pick a controller with a driver or HID profile your OS already supports to avoid middleware.

EMI in industrial environments

Capacitive touch is sensitive to noise from motors and relays. Industrial EMI design notes cover grounding, shielding and frequency hopping that keep touch stable on the factory floor.
