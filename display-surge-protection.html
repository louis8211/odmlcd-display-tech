Surge and Power Protection for Displays

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Transient sources

Lightning-coupled lines, inductive load switching and hot-plug can dump high-voltage transients into the supply. Without clamping they reach the driver IC.

Protection stack

  • TVS diode at the entry to clamp surges.
  • Fuse or polyfuse for sustained faults.
  • Common-mode choke and filter for conducted noise.
See also EMI and ESD protection.

Industrial supply

Specify the surge rating against the site; rugged industrial LCD programs often qualify to a defined transient level.
