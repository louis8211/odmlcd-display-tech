OLED Burn-in: Causes and Mitigation

Overview · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Why burn-in happens

Organic emitters age with use, so static, always-on elements (status bars, logos) dim faster than the rest, leaving a ghost. See what AMOLED is.

UI and drive strategies

Shift layouts periodically, dim static regions, use screen savers and balance average luminance. These slow burn-in dramatically for medical and control displays.

When OLED is still right

For low-light, high-contrast, non-static UI, OLED's image quality wins. For always-on fixed HMI, industrial TFT still leads on lifetime.
