LVDS vs eDP for Industrial Panels

Reference · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

What eDP adds

eDP (embedded DisplayPort) packets video over a 1-4 lane main link with a low-power AUX channel for config and touch. It carries higher resolution per lane than LVDS and is the default on modern laptop and SoC Silicon.

LVDS vs eDP

FactorLVDSeDP
LanesFixed pairs1-4 scalable
CableRobust, longShorter, tighter
EcosystemMature SBCsNew SoCs

When eDP fits industry

For industrial panels on a modern SoC with high resolution and a compact enclosure, eDP saves lanes and connectors. See the MIPI vs LVDS and interface primer for the full map.
