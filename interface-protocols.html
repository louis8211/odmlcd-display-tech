LCD Interface Primer: RGB, LVDS, MIPI DSI, eDP, SPI

Reference · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

The interface is the contract between your source (MCU, SBC, GPU) and the panel. Pick it wrong and nothing shows; pick it right and the rest of the design gets simpler. This is a practical, not exhaustive, guide.

Comparison

InterfaceSignal typeTypical useNotes
RGB (parallel)Parallel TTLSmall MCU panelsMany pins; short trace length
SPISerialTiny low-res displaysLow bandwidth, simple
LVDSDifferential serialIndustrial TFT 5–15.6"Robust, long cable, mature
MIPI DSIDifferential serialMobile/embedded, high resCompact, needs DSI source
eDPDifferential serialHigh-res panels, PCsStandardised, scalable

How to choose

  • Small embedded / MCU: RGB or SPI — keep it simple.
  • Industrial TFT with an SBC: LVDS is the safe, long-cable default.
  • High resolution or modern SoC: MIPI DSI or eDP.
  • Long cable runs in a cabinet: prefer differential (LVDS/eDP) over parallel RGB.
The interface drives controller board selection. For panel-level specs and available interfaces across our modules, see odmlcd.com or contact sales@odmlcd.com.
