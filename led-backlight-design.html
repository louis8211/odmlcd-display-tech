LED Backlight Design for Industrial LCDs

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Brightness and nits

Brightness is specified in nits (cd/m²). A desktop panel sits near 250-300 nits; sunlight-readable industrial displays often start at 800-1000 nits. More nits means more LED current, which drives the rest of the design.

Lifetime and thermal

LED output decays with temperature and hours. Industrial programs specify useful life at a defined temperature (e.g. 50% brightness after 30,000-50,000 h). Good thermal paths to the bezel or chassis keep the junction cool and protect that number.

Dimming methods

  • PWM dimming: simple, but low frequencies can flicker near cameras.
  • Constant-current / analog: smoother, no flicker, narrower range.
  • Local or zone dimming: rare on small industrial panels, used on large HMI.
