Display Cover Glass and Lens Design

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Strengthened glass

Cover lenses use chemically strengthened alkali-aluminosilicate glass, where ion exchange builds a compression layer that resists impact and scratches. A capacitive touch module stacks this lens above the sensor.

Thickness and touch

Lens thickness (0.7-2.0 mm) sets stiffness and touch sensitivity. Thicker glass is tougher but can need a stronger controller; pair with touch controller choice.

Surface treatment

AG etching or AR film on the lens cuts glare and reflection. See AG vs AR for the trade-offs.
