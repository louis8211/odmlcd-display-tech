LED Backlight Driver IC Selection

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Constant current and topology

LEDs need a regulated current, usually from a boost converter with a current sink per string. The driver sets brightness headroom - see backlight design.

Channels and dimming

Multi-string drivers must match channel current to avoid colour banding. PWM dimming is common; pick a frequency above camera flicker and below EMI trouble.

Thermal and protection

Good drivers add over-temperature, over-voltage and open-string protection, tying into the power budget and thermal path.
