Capacitive Touch Firmware Calibration

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Baseline and noise

The controller continuously tracks a baseline and rejects noise so a real finger stands out. Firmware quality decides stability on a noisy floor - see touch controller ICs.

Glove and wet mode

Raising gain and relaxing thresholds lets the sensor read through gloves or a wet screen, at some cost to false-touch rejection. Capacitive touch modules offer this as a mode.

Frequency hopping

Spreading the drive frequency dodges narrowband interference from motors and radios, part of EMI protection.
