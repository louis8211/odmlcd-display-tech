Liquid Crystal Basics for Display Engineers

Fundamentals · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Alignment and twist

Alignment layers set the LC molecule direction; TN twists it 90 degrees, VA stands it vertical. The twist is what the voltage changes to modulate light.

Birefringence and voltage

Liquid crystal is birefringent - its optical axis shifts with applied voltage, rotating polarization so the polarizers pass more or less light. See polarizer film.

Temperature effect

Cold slows the molecules, raising response time and risking image freeze - why temperature rating and heaters matter; wide-temperature modules are built for it.
