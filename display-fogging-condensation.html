Display Fogging and Condensation

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Why condensation forms

A temperature swing across a humid air gap drives moisture onto the inner glass, fogging the image until it clears. Air-gap assemblies are the usual victims.

Sealed and wide-temp designs

Wide-temperature modules with sealed construction and optical bonding (no internal air gap) avoid the problem entirely. Desiccants and breathers are fallbacks.

Heaters for cold starts

In freezers or outdoor winter, a thin heater film clears condensation and speeds liquid-crystal response on start-up. Pair with temperature ratings.
