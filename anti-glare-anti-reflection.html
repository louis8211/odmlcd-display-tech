Anti-Glare vs Anti-Reflection Coatings

Fundamentals · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Anti-glare (AG)

AG etches or textures the cover surface so reflections scatter instead of mirroring. It cuts harsh glare and is cheap, but slightly softens image sharpness. Common on sunlight-readable units.

Anti-reflection (AR)

AR is a thin optical film that cancels reflection at a wavelength band, keeping the image sharp. It is pricier and pairs well with optical bonding.

Which to choose

  • Rugged, gloved, outdoor: AG for diffuse glare control.
  • Precision, medical, indoor high-contrast: AR for clarity.
  • Both: AG+AR stack for tough, readable installs.
