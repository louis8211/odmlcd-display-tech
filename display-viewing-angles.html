LCD Viewing Angles: TN vs IPS

Fundamentals · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

TN vs IPS

TN panels are cheap and fast but shift colour and contrast off-axis. IPS industrial TFT holds colour and contrast across a wide cone, which matters when an operator reads the screen from an angle.

Polarity inversion and flicker

To avoid DC bias on the liquid crystal, controllers invert polarity each frame. Poor inversion shows as flicker or banding at certain angles - a sign of a weak drive scheme, not just the panel type.

Mounting angle

Specify the viewing cone against the real install: a panel mounted above eye level needs good downward viewing; a bench unit needs wide horizontal. Match the cone to the selection checklist.
