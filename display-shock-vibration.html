Shock and Vibration Resistance for Displays

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Where stress comes from

Transport, motors, relays and operator handling all shake the panel. Glass and bond lines are the weak points. See failure modes.

Bonding and frame design

Optical bonding removes the air gap that lets the cover glass flex and crack, and a rigid frame spreads load. Both raise shock tolerance.

Specifying the test

Ask for the vibration profile (frequency, g) and drop/shock rating your install sees, then qualify against it rather than a generic number.
