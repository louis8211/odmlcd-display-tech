Display Color Gamut and Accuracy

Fundamentals · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Gamut basics

Gamut is the set of colors a panel can show, referenced to sRGB, AdobeRGB or NTSC. A wider gamut shows more saturated color but needs content and calibration to use. See brightness and contrast.

Why it matters

For medical displays and inspection UI, consistent, calibrated color is safety-relevant. For general HMI it is mostly cosmetic.

Specifying color

Ask for the gamut coverage and a delta-E target if color matters. Otherwise a standard sRGB industrial panel is enough and cheaper.
