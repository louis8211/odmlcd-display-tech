OLED vs TFT LCD for Industrial Applications

Overview · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Contrast and response

OLED pixels emit their own light, giving true black and very fast response - excellent for low-light control rooms and high-contrast UI. TFT LCD needs a backlight, so black is merely dim.

Lifetime and burn-in

Organic emitters age and can burn in static UI elements - a real risk for always-on HMIs. Industrial TFT LCD modules are mature here, with specified lifetimes and no burn-in for fixed layouts.

Temperature and supply

OLED narrows at temperature extremes and long-term supply of a specific panel is harder to guarantee. TFT LCD spans wide-temperature ranges with steadier long-life sourcing - why many industrial programs still default to TFT.
