Industrial Display Mounting Options

Applications · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Open frame vs panel mount

Open frame drops the bare cell into your enclosure for a flush build; panel mount uses a gasket and clips from the front. Rugged industrial LCD options often support both.

Bezel and VESA

A front bezel protects edges and looks finished; VESA mounts suit arm or stand installs. Pick against the enclosure and service access you actually have.

Sealing for the environment

For washdown or dusty floors, specify gasket seating and an IP-rated front. This pairs with shock and vibration design for a durable build.
