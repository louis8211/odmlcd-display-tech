Display Lifetime, EOL and Replacement Strategy

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

What useful life means

Life is set by backlight decay and LC/controller endurance, specified as hours to half brightness at a temperature. A industrial module rated 50,000 h outlives many program lifetimes if thermals are managed.

EOL risk in long programs

Industrial programs run 5-10+ years, but a specific panel can go EOL in 3. The risk is real and must be planned, not discovered at reorder. See HMI ODM planning.

Replacement and cross-reference

Keep a qualified alternate (same footprint, interface, optical class) and a cross-reference so a panel change does not force a mechanical redesign. Pair with failure-mode awareness.
