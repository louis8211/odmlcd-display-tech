FPC and Flex Cable Design for Displays

Hardware · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Why FPC

Fine-pitch flex cable carries the parallel or serial link from the panel to the T-con and touch IC. Its thin profile suits tight industrial bezels.

Bend life and strain

Dynamic bends need a defined bend radius and strain relief; repeated flexing fatigues the copper. This matters as much as shock and vibration for field units.

Impedance and EMI

Display interfaces such as LVDS or eDP need controlled impedance; shield and ground the cable to limit pickup, as in EMI and ESD protection.
