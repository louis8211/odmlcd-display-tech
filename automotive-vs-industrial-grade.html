Automotive vs Industrial Grade Displays

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Grade tiers

Commercial grade targets 0 to 50 C; industrial grade targets wider temperature and longer life; automotive grade adds qualification (thermal cycles, vibration, AEC-Q) for the cabin. See temperature ratings.

Reliability differences

Automotive qualification is stricter and costlier than industrial. For factory floor use, wide-temperature industrial modules usually meet the need without automotive pricing.

Choosing for your program

Match the grade to the environment and certification your customer demands - do not over- or under-specify, both waste money.
