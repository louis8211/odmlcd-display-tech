RoHS, REACH and Display Compliance

Reliability · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Substance limits

Regulations cap lead, cadmium, mercury and halogen content in components and packaging. Compliant cells carry material declarations you can pass down the chain.

Supply chain proof

Keep declarations with the bill of materials so a customer audit is fast; this pairs with EOL planning for long programs.

Regional scope

EU and China RoHS plus REACH are the common bars for export; industrial modules are specified to meet them.
