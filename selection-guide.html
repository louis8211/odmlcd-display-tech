Industrial Display Selection Checklist

Reference · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

Start from the environment and the program life, not from a favourite panel. This checklist points to the module family that fits each requirement.

1. Environment

If your device…Start here
Sits in direct sunlightSunlight-readable / high-brightness
Runs outdoors or unheatedWide-temperature modules
Needs a sealed, rugged faceCapacitive touch + optical bonding
Is medical / lab equipmentMedical-grade displays

2. Size & shape

  • Standard rectangles 0.96–21.5" — broadest availability.
  • Round / bar-type for meters and appliances — see round & bar TFT.

3. Touch

Capacitive for modern UIs (glove/water options available); resistive where a stylus or gloved press is required. Bind it with the cover glass as one optically bonded stack.

4. Lifetime & supply

For industrial programs, confirm long-life supply (5+ years) and that the module + controller are validated together. This is where ODM programs earn their cost.
