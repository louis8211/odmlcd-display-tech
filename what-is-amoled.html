What Is an AMOLED Display — and When It Beats a TFT

Fundamentals · Companion to the article on odmlcd.com

AMOLED (Active-Matrix Organic Light-Emitting Diode) is a display where each pixel makes its own light. There is no backlight and no liquid-crystal shutter — when a pixel is "off", it is genuinely dark.

Key properties

  • True black & infinite contrast — off pixels emit nothing, so contrast is not limited by backlight leakage.
  • Thin & flexible substrate — OLED stacks can be built on plastic, enabling curved and very thin assemblies.
  • Fast response — no LC relaxation, so motion is clean.
  • Lower power for dark UIs — only lit pixels draw current.

AMOLED vs. industrial TFT LCD

FactorAMOLEDIndustrial TFT LCD
Brightness in sunlightLimited by emissive outputHigh with LED backlight (1000+ nits)
Operating lifeOrganic degradation, varies by useLong, stable for 5+ year programs
Cold / wide tempGoodExcellent with wide-temp options
Cost at volumeHigher for large sizesMature, competitive
For industrial and long-life programs, a high-brightness TFT with optical bonding is usually the safer baseline. See the full comparison on odmlcd.com/blog/what-is-amoled-display.
