Polarizer Film in TFT LCDs

Fundamentals · Part of the RONEN DISPLAY technical library

How polarizers work

Two polarizer films sit crossed above and below the cell. The liquid crystal rotates light between them, so voltage sets how much passes at each pixel - the basis of the image. See liquid-crystal basics.

Viewing angle and contrast

Polarizer angle and compensation film set the viewing cone and contrast - the mechanical side of viewing angles.

Optical film stack

Between the backlight and panel, diffuser, prism and reflectors shape the light; backlight design covers this stack.
